## Shared scope

Engineering is the primary audience, with potential use across the organization. The model spans product and system context down to components as useful. It references complete CAD information in PDM/PLM and work records in Jira rather than taking ownership of those external records.

## Modeling status

These use cases have model notes and **proposed** navigational trace links to the stakeholder need. They have not been broken down into approved system functions, requirements or verification cases, and native SysML cross-element relationships are not certified. Do not treat use-case completeness or implementation acceptance as proven.
