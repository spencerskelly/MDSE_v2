## Existing rule — verbatim

- **MOD-044** · Unreviewed · P1 — Note layout: frontmatter in governed order (type, subtype, id, uid, status, eaType, tags, sourceRole, primarySymbol, relationship fields), user zone, three blank lines, ## Source: EA, then ## SysML v2 always last with one fenced sysml block plus a one-line hint.
