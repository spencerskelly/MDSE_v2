## Existing rule — verbatim

- **WKB-022** · Active · P1 — The Workbench keeps structure, design types, internal structure, function, states and requirements apart. Structure shows parts only. Design types shows only what a definition specializes and what specializes it. Internal shows the parts with the ports each part's type offers (inherited ones included), the definition's own ports, and every connection, binding and flow with both ends resolved; an end that names a part or port that does not exist is marked. Function shows actions and States shows states, each drawn alone. Requirements shows the trace around the selected note. The Obsidian panel has the same lenses. (D-0041)
