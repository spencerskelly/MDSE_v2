## Existing rule — verbatim

- **WKB-017** · Active · P1 — The Edit lens Merge tab marks aliases and merges them in two steps. An alias is marked from the open note or from matches found between BOM parts and EA notes: the EA part number (`- PN:` or `- Part Number:`) equal to the BOM number, then the number inside the EA title, then a close name; only the first two with a single candidate are ticked for the person. Merge needs an alias, so nothing merges without being marked and reviewed. (D-0034)
