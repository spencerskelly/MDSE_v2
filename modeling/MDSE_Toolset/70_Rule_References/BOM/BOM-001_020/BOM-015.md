## Existing rule — verbatim

- **BOM-015** · Active · P2 — The importer reads the BOM from `.xlsx`, `.csv` or `.tsv` in the page. Two layouts are recognised by header names: flat (Parent Part Number, Child Part Number, Linenumber, Amount, Unit …) and redline (Number, CL1, CL2, level columns 0..7 or Structure). Part numbers are read as text. In a redline, unstruck text in a cell is current and struck text is the previous value; a part number struck with no replacement is a removed line. A sheet with no recognised header is refused with the reason. The redline layout was checked against the four real redlines (D-0040): the header may sit below a preamble, the level may be an unlabelled column just left of the part number, the number column may be `Part Number`, and the unit may be `Unit`, `UOM` or `Unit Of Measure` (when `Unit Of Measure` exists, `Unit` is the quantity). (D-0032, D-0040)
