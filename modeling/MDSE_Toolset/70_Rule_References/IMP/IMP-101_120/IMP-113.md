## Existing rule — verbatim

- **IMP-113** · Active · P1 — An Aggregation or Composition makes the child a usage in the whole, of the kind its two definitions are: part/part is `part`, action/action `action`, state/state `state`, item/item `item`, requirement/requirement `requirement`, part/port def `port`, use case/use case `use case`. The whole is the end marked composite or shared. Every connector is its own usage, even with the same two ends; it takes the child end's role name, else the child's name with `_2` suffixes, and the child end's multiplicity (none is invented). An EA Part or nested element of that type is matched to one connector and not repeated. A connector whose whole and part sit in one cycle of ownership is left out as OWNERSHIP_CYCLE. A part that is the child of an interface is AGGREGATION_PART_IN_INTERFACE (review). (Test_Vault_ approved aggregation docs 2026-10-17; D-0038)
